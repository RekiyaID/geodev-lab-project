 Project: Urban Growth: Environmental Impact in Abuja Assessment 

Part 2: Why it matters: 
Abuja has experienced recurring flooding events in recent years, with rapid urban growth and continuous development contributing significantly to the problem. The expansion of built-up areas, often accompanied by vegetation loss, increased impervious surfaces, and changes to natural drainage systems, has heightened the city’s vulnerability to flooding and other environmental impacts.

Part 5: What you would build
The end goal of the analysis is to assess how urban growth in Abuja has influenced environmental conditions, particularly flood risk, and identify areas where continued development may increase vulnerability to flooding. The key deliverables will include land-use/land-cover and urban growth maps, flood-conditioning factor maps, Sentinel-1-derived historical flood extents, a flood susceptibility map, and an urban growth–flood hotspot map showing existing and emerging risk areas. The findings will provide spatial evidence to support urban planning, development control, flood mitigation, and sustainable land-management decisions in Abuja.
